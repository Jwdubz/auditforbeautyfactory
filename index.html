What your site is costing you — Beauty Factory MedSpa —

These tweaks should help you land a few more clients through your website.

I put together an audit of the tweaks I'd make, plus a quick look at how your business shows up when someone searches for what you do. I went through beautyfactorymedspa.com the way someone in Las Vegas looking for a facial, Morpheus8 or Ultherapy would: search, open the site, read the treatment page, then call or book. The foundation is solid: 35 of the 36 pages I found load, every treatment page has a tap-to-call link to (702) 352-3560 and a booking button, and your homepage carries your address, phone and hours in a form Google can read. The leaks are in what the website template left behind: a phone link on your About page that dials a different number, two location cards for San Francisco and Los Angeles with placeholder text, 24 page titles that still end in the template's name, and a few links that go nowhere.

03

24 of your page titles still end in “Zen - Webflow Ecommerce website template”.

The title is the line Google shows as the blue link. All seven treatment pages have titles like “Morpheus8 - Zen - Webflow Ecommerce website template” and “Ultherapy - Zen - Webflow Ecommerce website template”, with neither your name nor Las Vegas in them. The same ending is on 6 blog posts, 3 blog category pages and 8 team pages, including Jessica's. Your homepage, About, Services, Contact, Promotions, Self Care Club, Team, Blog and Testimonials pages have titles of their own. In my Morpheus8 and Ultherapy searches, the pages that came back had titles like “Morpheus8 Las Vegas” and “Ultherapy Las Vegas”. I can't tell how much the title alone affects where your pages place.

04

A few links go nowhere or to the wrong place.

The “All articles” link on your homepage and the “Browse all posts” link on two blog posts go to /blog-pages/blog-v1, which returns a 404 page that says “We're redirecting” and that the site “just switched to a newer, better site”. One blog post's address is a full sentence with spaces in it (“/blog-pages/blog-posts/Discover the most effective spa treatments for melting a…”), which some apps and messages break when the link is shared. On your homepage, the YouTube and LinkedIn icons link to youtube.com and linkedin.com themselves, not to a page of yours.

05

Google has no sitemap for your site, and your business details are on the homepage only.

beautyfactorymedspa.com/sitemap.xml returns a 404 and your robots.txt is an empty file, so there's no list of pages for Google to start from. Your homepage carries business markup (MedicalBusiness) with your address, (702) 352-3560, info@beautyfactorymedspa.com and hours of Monday to Sunday 10 to 6. None of the seven treatment pages has markup of its own, and your Terms and Privacy pages have no headline and no description. Your Services page reuses the About page's description. I can't tell how many of your pages Google has listed.

What's working

Every treatment page has a tap-to-call link to (702) 352-3560 and a Schedule Booking button that goes to your SpaTime booking page. Your homepage has business markup with your address, phone, email and hours, and none of the 54 images on it is missing alt text. Both beautyfactorymedspa.com and the www address load, and every page names the address without www as its canonical, so Google gets one version. The directory listings I found show 5.0 from roughly 300 or more Google reviews.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Pages can be read

    35 of the 36 pages I found return 200. Every page names one canonical address, without www.

  • Found
    AI crawlers aren't blocked

    Your robots.txt is empty, so nothing there keeps GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, OAI-SearchBot or CCBot from reading the site.

  • Found
    Business markup on the homepage

    MedicalBusiness markup with your address, (702) 352-3560, info@beautyfactorymedspa.com, Monday to Sunday 10 to 6, your Facebook and Instagram, and your services.

  • Gap
    No sitemap

    /sitemap.xml and /sitemap_index.xml both return 404, and robots.txt has no Sitemap line.

  • Gap
    Load time on a simulated phone

    Lighthouse (simulating a slow phone connection) scored your homepage 63 out of 100, with the main content, the homepage video, finishing at 5.1 seconds and 4,499 KiB loaded; the video file is about 1.6 MB. The Morpheus8 page scored 54, finishing at 7.3 seconds. One run each, and a real phone on a fast connection will do better.

    Hours that disagree

    Your site and markup say Monday to Sunday 10 to 6. The Yelp listing I saw says Monday to Saturday 8:30 to 5 and closed Sunday. I can't tell which is current.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 23, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI answers say when asked directly. I didn't ask those assistants myself, so the searches above are a stand-in.
  • How many people search for these terms or how many visit your site.
  • Your Google listing itself: its reviews, photos, hours and phone number as Google shows it.
  • How your pages feel on a real phone. My speed numbers are a lab simulation of a slow phone, and I didn't test on a phone.

What I'd fix first

Fix the About page card so its phone and email links match what it shows, and delete the San Francisco and Los Angeles cards from the slider on all three pages. Give the 24 template-titled pages titles of their own, starting with the seven treatments, for example “Morpheus8 in Las Vegas | Beauty Factory MedSpa”. Point “All articles” and “Browse all posts” at your Blog page, give the long blog address a short one, and link the YouTube and LinkedIn icons to your own pages or remove them. Turn on Webflow's sitemap and add business markup to the treatment pages. Then make the hours on Yelp match the site, or the other way round. I can't promise where any of it will place you.
